## SMS register

The three disease classes were first taught only on photo captions
(`leaf_descriptions.jsonl`, written while looking at leaf images), while
`other` was the only class written the way a farmer texts. The classifier
learned caption-versus-SMS, which separates cleanly, instead of
disease-versus-not: on held-out SMS, recall was leaf_rust 47%, phoma 3%,
healthy 3%, and 203 of 203 errors went into `other`.

Two sources were added to fix it.

**`sms_en.jsonl`** — 314 messages generated by `scripts/gen_sms_en.py` across
five topics. Two of them are not diseases. The topic *coffee leaves that look
green and healthy* is mapped to `other`, not `healthy`: every message under it
is a yield complaint with healthy-looking leaves ("green and healthy, but I
pick very few cherries"). That is a real problem the model cannot diagnose, so
it belongs to a human — calling it healthy would answer "your coffee sounds
healthy, keep weeding" to someone who just lost half a harvest.

**`sms_gap_en.jsonl`** — 58 messages **written by hand**, not generated, for
two gaps the topics left: shoot-tip dieback (1 of 116 generated phoma messages
mentioned it, though it is a defining symptom) and genuinely healthy leaves
with no marks (the generator produced none). Marked `source: handwritten`.

Messages are grouped per topic so one topic spreads across train, calib and
test: blocks of eight for the generated file, four for the hand-written one,
which is small enough that eight put a whole register in a single split.
5 of 52 held-out SMS still have a close paraphrase in train (difflib ratio
>= 0.8), so held-out SMS accuracy is somewhat optimistic.

**`domain_pairs.jsonl` is not a test set.** Its 314 Luganda lines are
translations of the 314 English messages in `sms_en.jsonl`, so 230 of them
are now training rows. Quoting a score over all 314 measures the model
against its own training data. The honest figure is the 84 pairs whose
English landed in calib or test: 85.7% correct label through translation and
classification (leaf_rust 92%, phoma 81%, other 86%), and 78% on
`data/eval/hard_test.csv`.

## Translator

`Adom4600/opus-lg-en-coffee-ct2` — Opus-MT lg->en fine-tuned on SALT plus
agricultural SMS, **trained outside this repository, on Google Colab**, then
converted to CTranslate2 int8. There is no training script here; the Hub repo
is the artefact. 81 MB on disk, 2.2 s to load, 105 ms a message, chrF 65.0 on
`data/synthetic/domain_pairs.jsonl` (`python scripts/eval_translate.py`).

One direction only. Everything sent to a farmer is hand-written in
`data/templates.json`, where `lg_verified: false` marks the Luganda no native
speaker has checked yet.
