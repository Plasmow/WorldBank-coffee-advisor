* Dataset size: 24.8 MB
* Limits: 
    - coffee wilt disease not covered but it is not so much of a problem for a local mount Elgon deployment as there is no Robusta plantation there which coffee wilt disease is mainly attacking in Uganda
    - only leaves photo. Diseases on berries and stems are not represented.
    - pests, nutrient and water are also not covered
